Help

    

      

        
Griddy is a lightweight ASCII diagram editor. Draw boxes, lines, arrows and circles, drop in FIGlet text banners, paint with color, and import images as ASCII art.

        
Pick a tool from the toolbar, click and drag on the canvas, and use Select to move, resize, recolor or delete anything you've placed. Work is autosaved locally, and Undo/Redo cover every edit.
